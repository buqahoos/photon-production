About

Hi, I'm Mohamed.

I'm a compositor, 3D generalist and motion designer, and the founder of Photon Production.

For almost 10 years I've been creating visuals for brands, broadcasters and agencies, from full-CGI airline commercials and car spots to logo reveals and banking explainers. I'm based in Bahrain and work with clients worldwide.

My background is in architecture: I hold a degree in Architecture and a master's in Digital Arts, and I'm completing a PhD in Digital Design at the University of Kent (December 2026). That mix of spatial thinking and digital craft shapes every project, from ArchViz to motion design.

I like to work across the whole pipeline: shooting, modelling, animation, look development and final compositing. Keeping every stage in one pair of hands means the idea you start with is the one that reaches the screen.
